    What is confidence?

    It is the probability that the real value stays close to the forecast, within a per-variable tolerance (e.g. ±2 °C for temperature).

    It is calibrated: Certo checks its past forecasts against what really happened (weather stations, then 3 months of reanalysis). A 90% confidence is right about 9 times out of 10.

    Not to be confused with the chance of rain: "Rain from 05:00 · 85% chance" says whether it will rain, confidence says how reliable the forecast is.

    "uncal." (uncalibrated): not enough history yet to check this variable here. The percentage then only reflects agreement between models, which is less reliable.
