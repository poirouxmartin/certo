# Certo: feature ideas (2026-11-20)

Ideas only, nothing built. Impact (high/medium/low) and effort (S/M/L) are estimates. Martin picks.

## Quick wins

| Idea | Why | Impact | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moon phase on night icons and in the sun card | The night view already shows a moon; the real phase makes it informative | Medium | S |
| Best dry window today | "Dry 14:00-17:00" tag for a walk, run or bike ride, from the hourly rain chance | High | S |
| Next 2 hours rain bar in the header | 8 quarter-hour bars from the 15-min data already fetched | High | S |
| "Felt" temperature toggle in the header | Apparent temperature on demand | Low | S |
| Share today as an image card | Temperature, icon, place, confidence | Low | S |

## Trust and reasons to come back

| Idea | Why | Impact | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Yesterday's check" | What Certo forecast for yesterday versus what the station measured, one line per day; shows the bias learning at work and builds trust | High | M |
| Personal report | One tap "it is raining here / it is dry here", stored locally and used as an extra ground truth for the cell | Medium | M |
| Favourites at a glance | One row per saved place with icon, temperature now and rain chance, from the Today tab | Medium | M |
| Morning summary line in the widget | "Dry until 16:00, 14 deg max", refreshed at the first unlock of the day | Medium | M |

## Larger features

| Idea | Why | Impact | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Official warnings | Meteo-France vigilance, then MeteoAlarm for Europe, as a red tag in the header. Check the data licence and a keyless feed first | High | M |
| Air quality and pollen card | Open-Meteo air quality API (keyless) | Medium | M |
| Activity scores | Laundry drying, frost on the car, garden watering, each from existing variables | Medium | M |
| Rain-in-15-minutes alert | Background notifications got 1.6.7 flagged by Google Play Protect; only with a foreground or user-started mechanism, to validate first | High | L |
| Quick Settings tile and Wear OS tile | Current temperature and icon | Low | L |

## Not proposed

- No streaks, badges or reward loops for a weather app.
- No guilt notifications (dark patterns).
